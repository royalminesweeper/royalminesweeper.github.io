Privacy Policy — Neon Minesweeper

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Neon Minesweeper ("the app") is an offline arcade puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app stores

Your game statistics (runs played, best score, best level, best combo, levels cleared, daily run scores and streaks), settings and chosen theme are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner below the game controls, optional rewarded ads (for hints and to get extra time when the clock runs out) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from the IP address,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever required by law, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app submits your scores (your best run score and your daily run score) to Google Play Games leaderboards. Other players can see these scores together with your Play Games profile name and avatar. Sign-in, profiles and leaderboards are provided by Google; you can sign out or change your profile visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device. The game works fully without signing in. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
