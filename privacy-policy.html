Privacy Policy — Royal Minesweeper

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Royal Minesweeper ("the app") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app stores

Your game statistics (games played and won, best times, streaks), settings and chosen theme are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for hints and to continue after hitting a mine) and occasional full-screen ads between games. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from the IP address,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever required by law, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional one-time purchases (remove ads, themes) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which items you own, which it stores on your device so purchases work offline. Google Play's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
